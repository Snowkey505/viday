|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mixed | `2722.6` | `1985.5` | `-27.1%` | `-30.8%` | `-71.9%` | **kotlinx.serialization** |

| write | `1275.0` | `1300.3` | `2.0%` | `1.7%` | `-14.9%` | **Jackson 3** |
