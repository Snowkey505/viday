| mixed | `2783.6` | `2010.0` | `-27.8%` | `-32.8%` | `-70.6%` | **kotlinx.serialization** |

| write | `1385.3` | `1307.1` | `-5.6%` | `-20.6%` | `-30.6%` | **kotlinx.serialization** |
